# Sources for the IEEE 2030.5 models

The models in `src/interoperability/models/ieee2030_5/` are generated by that package's `generate_models.py` from the
IEEE 2030.5 `sep.xsd` schema of the standard's supplemental material, which is **not** in this repository (IEEE
copyright). Obtain the supplemental material of each edition from IEEE and point the `IEEE2030_5_SCHEMAS` environment
variable at a directory with one folder per edition named by year (`2013/`, `2018/`, `2023/`); the generator renders
the newest edition with xsdata and builds `editions.py` from all of them.

`enums.py` and `constants.py` here are the enumerations Eclipse VOLTTRON transcribed from the schema's documentation
(`services/core/IEEE_2030_5/ieee_2030_5/models/` at commit `804bed4ae5d957341600765c21a99bf3c1daea64`, Apache-2.0,
Copyright 2023 Battelle Memorial Institute; headers kept). The schema documents its enumerations in prose, not as
`xs:enumeration` facets, so they cannot be generated from it directly. The 2023 additions (`DERControlType2` bits,
`ConnectStatusType2`) are recorded in `editions.py` as documented bit positions, not yet as enumerations here.
